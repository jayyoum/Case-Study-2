Prediction

We predict T1 Complete will have the smallest rendezvous time since it has
the most edges, largest mean and max degree, and the smallest diameter. 
Since all robots are connected to each other, it is normal to expect this 
network to gather the quickest. T2 star has low edges and degree, but its 
diameter is low because there is one central robot connecting all others,
so we expect this network to be second, and ring and disk to take more time
to rendezvous. Despite similar statistics, disk will take longer than ring
due to the physical separation of the robots into two areas, though increasing
the radius by 0.4 will reduce rendezvous time slightly.
